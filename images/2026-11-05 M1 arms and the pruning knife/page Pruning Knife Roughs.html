Pruning knife roughs

Three key poses for one harvesting hit, drawn over the M1 character's real proportions (1.4 m tall), before anything is animated. The gesture comes from Dani's reference video, the knife from Dani's picture of a round knife, both of 5 November 2026.

The gesture

Screen Recording 2026-11-05 212626.mp4: a 7½-second ad for a "high-altitude pruning sickle". From 1.96 to 2.83 s, 15 stills a second: he raises the pole, hooks a branch and pulls it down. One loop takes about 0.75 s. The three keys are outlined. Only the movement is taken from it, not the tool.

The knife

Dani's round knife: a turned wooden handle with two grooves at its end, a metal ring, and a blade that bends out and curls round about three quarters of a circle. Its inside edge cuts. The blade is about 22 cm long, as long as the short handle. The long knife is the same knife on a longer handle. It is held so the curl's opening faces the plant and its back faces away. It appears in the hands when harvesting starts and goes when it stops.

Long knife, tall trees

Both hands on a handle about 1.9 m long, enough to reach into a 3.2 m puffball tree. Faint blue: the knife in the key before. Red dashes: where the blade goes next.

Short knife, small plants

The same three keys with one hand on the 22 cm handle, on a 1.38 m cone tree. The video has no short knife; its sideways swing at a sapling (5.23 to 5.37 s) is the nearest.

Timing for one hit

In the game, a hit falls every 0.45 s (DEMO_HARVEST_HIT_TIME), so the loop runs about 1.6 times faster than in the video. Ticks are frames at 30 a second. Red: the hit, when the plant shakes and drops its items.

Notes

  • The arms are wide sleeves in the cloak's red, widening to the cuff, with small hands, after Dani's drawings of 5 November 2026. The M1 character doesn't have them yet.
  • Small plants take the short knife, large plants the long one (Dani, 5 November 2026).
  • The figure, its cloak, head, collar and legs are drawn to the game's own sizes. Each sheet has a height ruler in metres.
  • The game's camera sits behind and above the player. In keys 1 and 2 the knife shows above the head, so the swing also reads from behind.
