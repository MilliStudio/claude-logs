Under the island

A slice through the edge of the island, from green land out past a grey tile. The depths come from the code and are drawn to scale. The tile widths are approximate.

  • The lid lies 0.8 m under the ground, under every tile except where clouds hang. It is drawn after the tiles, so it covers everything below it, hidden tiles included.
  • Under the clouds, the lid has a hole down to the core, with dark walls round it, so the clouds can hang lower. The clouds are drawn after the lid, so they still show.
  • Clouds start at the green edge tiles and reach 1.5 tiles out. Their tops lie 0.75–1 m down. Grey tiles count as void to them, and none lie on or beside risen water.
  • Tiles' sides end 1.3 m down, 0.5 m below the clouds' tops, so where clouds lie, the sides disappear into them.
  • The core is a dark sphere 4 m under the ground.
  • A hidden tile is the whole tile, pushed 5.5 m down into the core: 4 + 0.5 + your map's highest ground (1 m). Things standing on it are switched off until it rises.
