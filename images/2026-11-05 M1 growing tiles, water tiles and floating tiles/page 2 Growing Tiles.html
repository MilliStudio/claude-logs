Growing tiles

What I understood from your answers, the pictures you asked for, two new points I found, and what I'd do to build it.

What I understood

  • Every tile is a column rooted inside the core. A hidden tile is a stub in there. Unlocked, its top grows up to its height, in the same wave as now, and what stands on it rides up with the top.
  • Each tile has a height slider in the editor: steps of 0.5 m, from the lowest (deep water's depth) up to 20 m. The map stays as it is, and things are placed on tiles as now.
  • The sides fade into darkness from one depth for every tile, whatever its height. No tile's top may go below where the fade starts, so a top is never darkened.
  • There's no lid. What you see below the tiles is the tiles themselves fading into darkness.
  • "Unhappy" is a tile not yet revived. It has no grass until it's revived.
  • Clouds only surround the outer sides of revived tiles, as now. Unhappy edge tiles get none, and their sides just fade into darkness.
  • The water is its own object, filling the gap between the tiles round it, with a face wherever a tile is missing. Its bed shows through it.

1. The water in its ring of tiles

The same pond tile, seen from above and to the south, with the tiles round it as columns. The water is the see-through blue block with the bright outline. Below it you see its bed, which is the top of its own tile's column.

6 tiles round it: the water has one face, its top.

5 tiles, the front one missing: the top, plus the side face where the tile is missing.

New with heights: the front tile set to −1 m, below the water's top. The side face shows only above that tile.

Why I said the faces could break: I overstated it. The faces broke on 4 November because of the lid. The lid was painted over everything below it, and the water, drawn after it, wasn't drawn wherever the lid lay behind it. The fix added a hidden copy of the lid to sort that out. With the lid gone, the water is drawn like any see-through object, and only the tiles in front of it hide it. The only risk is taking those lid rules out cleanly, and I'll test the water on your state, as before.

2. Where the fade starts

Every column fades between the same two depths, so sides next to each other always match. The lowest top sits one step above where the fade starts.

3. The darkness follows the sky

The core is painted with the colour of the sky behind it, so at night it's dark blue, and in daylight it's sky blue. The columns' fade has to end in that same colour at every moment, or their bottoms show as a dark band whenever the sky is lighter. I'll make the fade use the sky's colour, as the core does.

4, 6 and 7

  • 4, the water: you're right. The water already takes the shape of the tiles round it, and columns don't change that. Only two things differ. The soil slab under its bed becomes its own tile's column, down into the core. And a tile can now be lower than the water's top (picture 1, right).
  • 6, clouds as the island grows: with your Q2 answer it's no problem. Clouds stay round revived tiles, as now. When the green spreads, the clouds on the old edge end up inside the new columns, hidden, and new ones form at the new edge.
  • 7, a tile growing under the player: I meant the player, not the look. If you float where a hidden tile grows, its top comes up through you. Today's rising tiles do the same, so this change adds nothing new. I withdraw it.

New points I found

Q6

Beside an edge tile lower than 0 m, the clouds drop to 0.8 m under its top, and so does the level the player floats at in the void. Everywhere else they stay 0.8 m under 0 m, even beside tall tiles, so falling off a 20 m cliff still lands you in the clouds. I recommend this.

Q5

Water tiles get no height slider for now. A pond stays where ponds are today: its top 0.4 m under 0 m and its bed 1.65 m under that, the deepest point. If water tiles did get heights, every tile of one pond would need the same one, or the water's top would step. I recommend no slider for now.

Tall columns can hide the player. A tile up to 20 m high between the camera and the player hides them, as walls and giant flowers already can. Your decision of 4 November says nothing marks the player when that happens. I'd change nothing unless you want something.

What I'd do to build it

  1. Columns: every tile's sides reach from its top down into the core, instead of stopping 1.3 m down.
  2. Growing: a hidden tile's top waits inside the core. When it's unlocked, only the top moves up and the sides stretch, with the same wave and the same 0.7 s. What stands on it rides up with it.
  3. The fade: one fade for every tile, from −2.5 m down to the sky's colour at the core (−4 m). Unhappy tiles without grass.
  4. The lid goes: its holes, its walls, its hidden copy and the drawing rules built around it.
  5. Heights: a height slider in each tile's box in the editor, in 0.5 m steps from −2 m to 20 m. The map drawing stays, with heights saved beside it, as the flowers' settings are. Old maps still read: . : ; = become 0, 0.5, 1 and 2 m.
  6. Water: the same object, with a side face wherever a tile is missing or lower than its top.
  7. Corner pieces: wedge-shaped columns that grow and fade the same way.
  8. Clouds: the same rule, round revived edge tiles, against their outer sides, and lower beside low tiles if you say yes to Q6.
  9. Tests: the tests, then the game in a hidden browser on your state: the grey island, a pond partly risen, seen from the bank and low down, by day and by night.
  10. Saving: a new version, the README, a commit and a push. You get it with Update M1 testing. After that, the decisions for Tessera Records, with your yes.
